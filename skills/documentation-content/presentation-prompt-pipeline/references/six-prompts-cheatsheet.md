# Source infographic — "How to build a presentation using AI"

Cyberman AI fat-loss-style infographic: *6 powerful prompts. From idea to
impactful deck.* Transcribed verbatim below; the operational version (prompt
templates + gates + linter) lives in `../SKILL.md`.

---

**1. THE BLUEPRINT**
- 🎯 The ONE message
- 👥 Audience & what they care about
- 🤍 3 emotional angles
- 📊 Slide-by-slide flow
- ▁▃▅ Ideal slide count
*Visual: clipboard with ticked items.*

**2. THE STRUCTURE**
- 📋 Slide-by-slide outline
- 🎯 One-line purpose
- 🙂 What they should feel
- 📈 Build like a story
*Visual: winding road with three map pins.*

**3. THE STORY FRAME**
- 🪝 Hook – grab attention
- ⚠️ Problem – the pain
- 💡 Insight – the surprise
- 🔧 Solution – what to do
- 🛡️ Proof – one stat/example
- ✅ Takeaway – one action
*Visual: open book.*

**4. THE VISUAL DIRECTION**
- 🖼️ Visual that carries emotion
- 🥧 Right chart for data
- ▦ Icon or grid for lists
- 🎨 3-color palette
- Aa Font pairing
*Visual: three colour cards (cream, orange, black) fanned out.*

**5. THE CONTENT**
- ✏️ Punchy title (6 words max)
- ≡ 3–5 bullets under 10 words
- 💬 One speaker note
- 👥 Tailored to audience
- 🎙️ Right tone
*Visual: document with chat bubble.*

**6. THE BUILD**
- ⬇️ Full presentation file
- **B** Bold titles, tight bullets
- 🔈 Speaker notes on every slide
- ✓ Clean, logical flow
- 🎁 Ready to present
*Visual: laptop with play button and download icon.*
